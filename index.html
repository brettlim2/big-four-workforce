    Next-data modules (not in this snapshot)

    • Vacancies / demand — would support hiring-market pressure and competitor demand; requires job-posting or ATS feeds.
    • Pay and benefits — would support compensation positioning; requires salary survey or offer data.
    • Diversity outcomes — would support equity and inclusion decisions; requires demographic fields with clear consent and coverage.
    • Productivity / delivery outcomes — would test whether tech share or hub density correlates with delivery; requires operational KPIs.
    • Skills over time — would support reskilling plans; requires longitudinal skill tags rather than a single title snapshot.
